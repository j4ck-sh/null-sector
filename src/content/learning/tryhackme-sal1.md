## Areas of Learning

The following sections describe the principal subjects I have been studying. They are thematic summaries rather than an exhaustive or independently verified syllabus.

### Security Operations

Security operations involves monitoring systems, identifying potentially suspicious behaviour, and supporting the investigation of security events.

An important part of my learning has been understanding that effective analysis depends on process as well as technical knowledge.

A security alert is a starting point for investigation, not automatically proof that a security incident has occurred.

### Alert Triage

Alert triage involves assessing the available information to determine what deserves further investigation.

Relevant considerations can include:

- What triggered the alert.
- Which systems, accounts, or assets are involved.
- Whether supporting evidence is available.
- How the activity fits its surrounding context.
- What additional information is needed before drawing conclusions.

The emphasis is on developing a reasoned assessment rather than relying on an alert label alone.

### Log Analysis

Logs can provide important evidence about activity across systems, applications, and networks.

Studying log analysis has reinforced the importance of understanding what a particular data source can establish—and what it cannot.

A useful investigation may require comparing multiple sources, considering timestamps, and accounting for missing or incomplete information.

### Incident Investigation

Incident investigation requires an organised approach to collecting context, examining evidence, and recording findings.

The areas I want to continue strengthening include:

- Forming clear investigative questions.
- Following evidence across related events.
- Recording the reasoning behind conclusions.
- Identifying uncertainty and alternative explanations.
- Producing concise technical documentation.

These are ongoing learning objectives, not claims that I have independently demonstrated each skill in a professional incident-response environment.
